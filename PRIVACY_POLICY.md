# Privacy Policy for Fluent Calculator

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Fluent Calculator is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

### 1. Data Collection
Fluent Calculator does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage telemetry, or sensitive user information.

### 2. Local Storage
All application data (including calculation history, stored memory numbers, custom settings, and theme preferences) is stored strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your computer and can be cleared at any time from the app settings or history panel.

### 3. Network Access
Fluent Calculator does not require an active internet connection to perform standard mathematical calculations, programmer bitwise operations, unit conversions, or date computations.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Fluent Calculator contains no age-inappropriate content, tracking, or advertisements and is suitable for users of all ages.

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project's repository.
